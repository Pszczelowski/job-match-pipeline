Project

Job-match pipeline

A pipeline that collects job alerts, drops what does not fit, scores the rest against a fact file of my CV and shows a daily top 10. Every CV and cover letter is drafted from that file only, checked against it, and approved by me before anything is used. It runs on my own computer and sends nothing to employers.

The demo is a frozen, read-only snapshot of its web app. All jobs, companies and texts in it are made up, and nothing you do there is saved.
